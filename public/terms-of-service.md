## 2. Service and prototype status

GradLink demonstrates a proposed connection between students and companies through task listings and applications. Email/password registration and sign-in use Supabase Auth. Google sign-in is available when configured. Professional and Advanced student setup demonstrates private Supabase CV storage.

Task listings, applications, company information, certificates, verification records, and portions of the profile/workflow may remain in browser storage or demo state. Server-side authorization and the full CV-to-application sharing flow are not yet implemented. Demo content and UI labels are not proof of real users, qualifications, company verification, work completion, or payment. Presentations should use fictional/demo data only.

## 4. Student responsibilities

Students should provide accurate education, skill, portfolio, CV, and application information; have the right to submit all materials they upload; and ensure work they provide complies with law and third-party rights. Students should review any proposed work-history information before adding it to a CV. The intended CV feature is not yet fully wired into the application-submission and employer-access workflow.

## 5. Company responsibilities

Companies must be authorized to act for the organization they name, describe work and budgets accurately, and not post unlawful, deceptive, discriminatory, fraudulent, unsafe, or exploitative tasks. Companies must not request passwords, one-time codes, unnecessary identity documents, or upfront applicant fees. A “verified” label must only be used after a real, documented review process; current demo labels do not verify actual companies.

## 6. Tasks, applications, and payment

Students and companies should agree directly on scope, deliverables, deadlines, payment amount and currency, payment method, intellectual-property terms, and dispute handling before work begins. GradLink does not currently process, hold, escrow, transfer, or guarantee payments. A displayed budget or company-recorded payment status does not prove that payment occurred. GradLink does not guarantee a listing, application, interview, hiring outcome, work quality, completion, or payment.

Users retain rights they hold in content they submit. **[Operator and counsel: define the limited license, if any, needed to store and display profile, listing, application, and CV content.]** Users should not upload content they have no right to share.
