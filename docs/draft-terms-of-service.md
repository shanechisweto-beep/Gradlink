# GradLink ZW Terms of Service — Working Draft

**Draft only — not approved for publication.** The service currently behaves as a browser prototype with demo data and unconnected account-management, payment, messaging, and moderation capabilities. This draft needs operator details, actual processes, and Zimbabwe legal review before it can be offered as binding terms.

By using GradLink, a user would agree to these terms and the Privacy Notice, subject to applicable law. Do not publish until the operator and a reliable notice/acceptance flow are confirmed.

## 2. Service status and limitations

GradLink is intended to connect Zimbabwean students/graduates and companies through task listings and applications. The inspected application is a prototype: much of its marketplace information is stored only in the browser; demo records appear in the interface; messaging, CV uploads, in-app payment processing, account deletion/export, and secure server-side authorization are not implemented. A listing, rating, status, or “verified” label in the prototype is not independently validated evidence of a real person, employer, qualification, job, payment, or outcome.

GradLink does not hold, transfer, escrow, or guarantee task payments in this prototype. A displayed budget or “payment recorded” status is not proof that funds were paid. Users should agree payment terms directly and take appropriate precautions. **[Operator must decide and legally review the role GradLink will take in any future payment/marketplace service.]**

## 3. Accounts

Users should provide accurate information, protect their sign-in credentials, and use only accounts they are authorized to control. Do not impersonate another person or create fraudulent qualifications, company records, or identity information. The current app does not provide full account recovery, account deletion, or session-management controls; these must be implemented before these terms are relied upon for a production service.

## 4. Student conduct and qualifications

Students must submit truthful profile, education, skill, portfolio, and application information; have rights to submit any work or material they provide; meet deadlines they accept; and not copy another person’s work or misrepresent results. Do not upload information or documents without authority to share them.

## 5. Company conduct and listings

Companies must be authorized to post on behalf of the named organization, describe the work and budget accurately, disclose material requirements, and not post unlawful, deceptive, discriminatory, fraudulent, unsafe, or unrelated listings. Do not request upfront fees, bank passwords, one-time codes, unnecessary identity documents, or off-platform activity intended to scam or exploit applicants. Do not describe a company as verified unless GradLink has actually completed a documented verification review under a published process.

## 6. Applications, work, reviews, and disputes

Students and companies are responsible for agreeing scope, deliverables, deadlines, payment amount/currency, payment method, intellectual-property terms, and dispute handling before work begins. GradLink’s prototype does not mediate payment disputes or guarantee hiring, work quality, completion, or payment. Ratings and feedback should be honest, relevant, and non-abusive; users may not manipulate reviews or retaliate through false claims.

## 7. Messaging

The inspected prototype has no in-app chat or messaging feature. Do not rely on these terms as a description of a current messaging service. If messaging is introduced, GradLink should publish rules for abuse reporting, blocking, moderation, access to message content, and retention before launch.
